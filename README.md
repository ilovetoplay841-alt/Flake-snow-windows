# ❄️ Flake

**Flake** brings realistic, interactive snow right to your desktop without getting in the way of your work. 

Snowflakes naturally accumulate on your open windows, taskbar, Dock, and widgets—and even move along with your windows when you drag them. Clicks pass right through the snow, and you can pause the snowfall in a single click whenever you need to focus.

## ✨ Key Features
* **Realistic Snow Accumulation:** Snow settles on window borders and screen edges.
* **Interactive Tools:** clear everything with a shovel, or roll snowballs to build snowmen right on your desktop.
* **Custom Weather & Wind:** Switch between light snow, steady snowfall, or a blizzard, and control wind direction and speed.
* **Snowplow & Festive Extras:** Call a snowplow (tractor, snowmobile, or custom image) to clear snowdrifts, or add glowing garlands to your windows.
* **Lightweight & Non-Intrusive:** Optimized to use minimal system resources and automatically pauses over fullscreen games and videos.
